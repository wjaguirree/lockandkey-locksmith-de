Questions

Residential questions from Blades

How does rekeying work for a house in Blades?

Rekeying means we change the internal pins of the lock cylinder so a different key operates it. For a Blades home we remove the cylinder, replace or rearrange the pins to match a new key, then reinstall the cylinder on site. Rekeying keeps your existing hardware while preventing old keys from working.

Can you repair the old deadbolt on my Blades house, or do I need a new lock?

We inspect the deadbolt cylinder, bolt assembly and the door frame to see if repairs are feasible. In Blades homes we often find corroded internal parts or a worn keyway that can be rebuilt or replaced while keeping the existing lock body. If internal damage is extensive, we will explain replacement options and match a compatible hardware finish.

What should I expect during a lock replacement at my Blades house?

We bring the tools and parts needed and work at your door. At a Blades house that often means checking the door jamb and frame for wear, choosing compatible hardware for older doors, and fitting a deadbolt or latch so it operates smoothly. We secure the door hardware and leave the area tidy.

Can you unlock a front door in Blades if I've locked keys inside?

We can attempt a non-destructive entry on typical residential front doors in Blades when the lock and door condition allow. Our mobile van carries tools commonly used for lockouts and replacement parts. We will assess the lock on site and explain options, including whether a new lock is advisable based on wear or damage.
