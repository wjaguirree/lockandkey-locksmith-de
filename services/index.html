Services

Everything we do, and where.

24 services in four groups, all of them carried out at your address across fifty towns in Kent and Sussex County. There is no counter to visit and nothing to drop off: the van leaves Georgetown with the stock the job needs.

If you are not sure which one applies, the categories below group them by the kind of door. Emergency work is for a door that will not open right now. Residential work covers the locks on a home you own or rent. Automotive work is keys, fobs and ignitions at the roadside. Commercial work is the hardware a business depends on. Every service page lists the 50 towns it is available in, and every town page lists the services we bring there.
