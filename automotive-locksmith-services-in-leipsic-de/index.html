Questions

Automotive questions from Leipsic

Can you replace a modern key fob if I lost mine in Leipsic?

We can replace many modern key fobs by programming a new unit to your vehicle using the tools in our van. When you contact us from Leipsic, tell us the vehicle make, model and year and whether the key had a remote or proximity fob so we bring compatible parts. We cannot guarantee programming for every model.

What kinds of car keys can you duplicate for Leipsic vehicles?

We can duplicate traditional metal blade keys, many remote fobs, and a range of transponder keys used in passenger cars. For Leipsic vehicles with older or basic ignition systems a straight cut is often enough. For remotes and chipped keys we verify the key type on site and bring the right equipment from our van to make a working duplicate when possible.

Can you program a replacement car key fob at my Leipsic house?

Yes, we can program replacement car key fobs at your Leipsic location. We bring the necessary programming equipment and work from our van on site, so you don't need to go anywhere. Tell us the vehicle make, model, and year when you call so we can bring compatible tools and any required blank fob types.

Can you make and program a transponder key for my car in Leipsic?

We make and program transponder keys at your vehicle in Leipsic, working at driveways, roadsides, or near the waterfront. We carry the equipment to match many vehicle makes and program the key to the vehicle's immobilizer. If a specific model has factory restrictions, we will explain what options are available before starting.
