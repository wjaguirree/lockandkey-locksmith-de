We bring a selection of replacement locks and cylinders sized for common residential doors, key blanks, rekeying kits, specialized mailbox tools, and compatible smart lock hardware when requested. Questions

Residential questions from Millville

What does lock rekeying actually involve?

Rekeying is the process of changing the internal pins in a cylinder so a different key operates the lock. We remove the cylinder from the door, reconfigure the pin stack to match a new key, then reinstall and test the cylinder on site. Rekeying preserves the existing lock hardware while giving you a new keying system.

Can you repair locks on rental properties in Millville?

Yes, we repair locks on rental properties in Millville. We work with deadbolts, keyed knobs, latch assemblies, and common multi-unit entry hardware used by landlords and property managers. We carry parts in our van so we can assess the problem on site and make repairs suited to the door, frame, and lock type present at the Millville rental.

What types of locks can you replace on a Millville rental cottage?

We replace standard single-cylinder deadbolts, keyed knobs, lever sets and many patio or sliding door locks commonly found on rental cottages in Millville. We bring parts and hardware on the van and match finishes to the existing door when possible. If a specialty lock is required, we discuss options and install the one that fits the door and tenant needs.

Can you unlock my front door in Millville if I lost my keys?

We can come to your Millville address in our mobile van and assess the lock on-site. We carry tools to open common residential deadbolts and knob locks without damaging the door in many situations. If the lock or door condition requires different handling, we will explain options and proceed with your approval.
