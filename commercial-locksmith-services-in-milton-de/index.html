Commercial questions from Milton

Can you install keyless entry on an older wooden door in Milton?

Yes. We assess the door, frame and existing hardware before recommending a compatible keypad or smart lock that fits an older wooden door without excessive modification. In Milton many homes have irregular door thicknesses and historic trim, so we match hardware that preserves the look while providing electronic access control and secure mounting.

How does a master key system work for a small business on Milton's Main Street?

A master key system lets a single higher-level key open several locks while subordinate keys open only individual doors. For a Main Street shop in Milton, this means the owner can carry one key for the shop and storage while employees have keys for their work areas only. We review existing locks and suggest compatible cylinders and keying schedules.

What is a panic bar and why would a Milton business need one?

A panic bar is a horizontal push device mounted on an exit door that lets people leave quickly without turning a knob. In Milton, businesses with public entrances, community halls and rental properties often choose panic bars to meet occupant safety needs and to provide a simple, familiar means of egress for customers and guests during an emergency.

Can you replace commercial storefront locks on historic buildings in Milton?

Yes. We handle replacement of commercial storefront locks for historic buildings in Milton. We assess the existing door and frame on site, recommend compatible hardware that preserves the look of the storefront, and fit locks that work with original trim and recessed entries. We carry common commercial hardware in our mobile workshop and install at the location.
