Can you extract a broken key from a residential deadbolt in Dover?

Yes. We extract broken keys from residential deadbolts in Dover and surrounding Kent County neighborhoods. We bring tools and parts to work on your lock in place, aiming to remove the broken section without damaging the cylinder when possible. If the cylinder needs replacement, we can discuss compatible options on site.

Do you handle broken keys in car ignitions or trunk locks in Dover?

We do work on vehicle ignitions and trunk locks in Dover and nearby towns like Camden and Wyoming. Some ignitions require specialized tools and assessment before work begins. We examine the key fragment and lock, explain available options, and proceed with extraction or advise on next steps if replacement is necessary.

After you extract the broken key, can you cut a new key or advise on lock security?

After extraction we can often cut a replacement key or recommend a compatible key blank and lock solution. We carry common blanks and can advise on whether a cylinder change, rekey, or upgraded hardware would suit your property. If a new key or parts are needed, we discuss choices before proceeding.
