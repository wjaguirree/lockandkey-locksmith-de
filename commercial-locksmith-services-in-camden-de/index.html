Questions

Commercial questions from Camden

What kinds of access control systems work best for older homes in Camden?

Older homes in Camden often have wood-frame doors and historic door frames that need careful fitting. We recommend access control components designed for retrofit installation, such as wireless keypads or battery-powered smart locks and electric strikes sized for the existing frame. We assess the door and frame on site and recommend options that avoid unnecessary modification while meeting your security and use needs.

What is a master key system and would it work for a Camden rental property?

A master key system lets one key open multiple locks while individual tenants keep separate keys for their units. For a Camden rental property with several small apartments or a duplex near Main Street, it reduces the number of keys managers carry and isolates tenant access. We assess your existing cylinders and recommend compatible cores and keying levels for the building.

Will a panic bar fit the older wood storefront doors on Main Street in Camden?

Many panic bars are adaptable to older wood storefront doors, but each installation requires inspection. We assess door thickness, stile construction and existing lock hardware before recommending a model. For Camden storefronts, we select bars that preserve the door appearance while meeting functional needs and ensuring smooth operation with the existing frame and thresholds.

What commercial hardware services do you provide for businesses in Camden?

We install and repair commercial door hardware including exit devices, mortise and cylindrical locks, door closers, and panic hardware. For Camden properties we also handle keyed-alike systems and master key setups, retrofit existing doors for new hardware, and replace worn cylinders or strike plates to improve security and usability for employees and customers.
