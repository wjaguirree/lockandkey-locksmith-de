Automotive questions from Lewes

Can you replace a modern key fob for my car in Lewes?

We carry the equipment in our van to cut and program many modern key fobs on site. For a Lewes location we will come to your vehicle and handle replacement and programming at the curb or driveway. We cannot guarantee results for every make and model, but we will assess your vehicle and proceed using the correct tools.

Can you duplicate a key for my car while I'm staying in a Lewes rental?

We can duplicate many types of car keys for customers in Lewes rental properties. Being mobile, we work from your curb or driveway rather than a shop. Tell us the vehicle make, model and whether the key has a transponder or remote function so we can bring the proper blanks and programming tools for Lewes rental locations.

Can you program a car key fob in Lewes for my late-model vehicle?

Yes. We program many late-model fobs using on-vehicle diagnostics from our mobile van. Tell us the vehicle make, model year, and whether you have an original fob or only a blank. We handle programming on site in Lewes at your driveway, parking area, or other permitted location, and we can confirm compatibility over the phone at (302) 271-0155.

My transponder key stopped working for my car parked near the Lewes Ferry terminal - what can you do?

We come to the vehicle in Lewes and start with a diagnosis of the key and the car's immobilizer system. In many cases we can cut a new key and program the transponder on-site, but the exact possibility depends on the vehicle make, model and factory security settings. Have the vehicle and proof of ownership ready when we arrive.
