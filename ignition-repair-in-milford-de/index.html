Ignition Repair in Milford

What signs of ignition failure should I look for in my Milford vehicle?

Common signs include a key that turns loosely, a key that won't turn, unusual resistance, or electrical accessories that don't power on when the key is turned. In Milford we see these issues in both older downtown cars and farm trucks. Note when the problem started and any recent work on the steering column to help diagnose.

Can you replace an ignition on a vehicle parked at my Milford rental property?

Yes. We perform ignition replacements and repairs at customer locations across Milford. We work from our mobile van and bring the necessary tools and parts for many common ignitions. Provide the vehicle make, model, and year beforehand so we can bring compatible replacement parts and keys whenever possible.

Will replacing the ignition affect my car's security system or keys?

Replacing an ignition can involve reprogramming transponder keys or pairing new keys with the vehicle's immobilizer. In Sussex County and in Milford we handle both mechanical cylinders and electronic key systems, but outcomes depend on the vehicle's make and model. Tell us the key type and any alarm or factory immobilizer details before we arrive.
